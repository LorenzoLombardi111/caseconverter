Title Case Converter Online — Free Instant Tool

Title Case is a capitalization style where the first letter of each word is written in uppercase and the remaining letters are lowercase. For example, "the quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog" becomes The Quick Brown Fox Jumps Over The Lazy Dog. Title Case is one of the most commonly used formatting styles in the English language, appearing in book titles, newspaper headlines, email subject lines, and presentation slides.

Writers, editors, marketers, and students use Title Case daily. Blog post titles, landing page headings, and social media captions all benefit from consistent Title Case formatting. Getting it right matters because inconsistent capitalization looks unprofessional and can confuse readers. A Title Case converter eliminates guesswork and ensures every heading follows the same pattern.

When to Use Title Case

Title Case is the standard for headings and titles in most English-language publications. The Associated Press (AP) Stylebook and the Chicago Manual of Style both recommend Title Case for headlines and book titles, though they differ on which small words (like "the," "and," "of") should remain lowercase. When in doubt, capitalizing every word is a safe default that freecaseconverter.app handles instantly.

Use Title Case for article headlines, book and chapter titles, email subject lines, course names, presentation slide titles, and navigation menu items. In marketing, Title Case headlines tend to perform well because they visually stand out and convey importance. However, avoid using Title Case for body text — that role belongs to Sentence case.

Code Examples

JavaScript

// Convert a string to Title Case in JavaScript
function toTitleCase(str) {
  return str.replace(/\w\S*/g, function(word) {
    return word.charAt(0).toUpperCase() + word.substr(1).toLowerCase();
  });
}

console.log(toTitleCase("the quick brown fox"));
// Output: "The Quick Brown Fox"

Python

# Python has a built-in title() method
text = "the quick brown fox"
print(text.title())
# Output: "The Quick Brown Fox"

# For more control, use a custom function
def to_title_case(s):
    return " ".join(word.capitalize() for word in s.split())

print(to_title_case("hello world from python"))
# Output: "Hello World From Python"

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Title Case?

Title Case is a capitalization style where the first letter of every word is capitalized. For example, "the quick brown fox" becomes "The Quick Brown Fox".

When should I use Title Case?

Title Case is used for headings, article titles, book titles, email subject lines, and presentation slides. Style guides like AP and Chicago both recommend Title Case for headlines, though they differ on which small words to capitalize.

What is the difference between Title Case and Sentence case?

Title Case capitalizes the first letter of every word (The Quick Brown Fox), while Sentence case only capitalizes the first letter of each sentence (The quick brown fox). Title Case is for headings; Sentence case is for body text.
